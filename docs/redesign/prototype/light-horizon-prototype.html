Motion

  • Bloom sheetThe add sheet opens out of the button you pressed.
  • Rows rise inPurchases and friends arrive one by one.
  • Tab lineA glowing line slides between Today and Circle.
  • Dial rippleA ripple leaves the stop each time a digit lands.
  • Coin burstSmall particles on save, confetti when the day is finished.
If you choose one
  1. Theme and type. Colours, Geist fonts and spacing as Compose tokens, with light and dark from one definition.
  2. Hero and list. The centred total with its horizon, then the ruled purchase list and the tab bar.
  3. Add sheet and dial. The bloom-out sheet, ruled keypad and the existing rotary dial restyled.
  4. Finish and effects. The stamp, coin burst and confetti, each checked on the emulator.
