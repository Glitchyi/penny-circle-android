Scope and provenance
This inventory follows the existing release package dependency report: 119 external Maven coordinates, excluding test/debug-only dependencies and local :core. Fifty-one license/notice entries were copied from the exact resolved AAR/JAR archives, including nested classes.jar files, before app packaging exclusions. Some archives do not supply a separate notice. The inventory is based on artifact/POM metadata and authoritative upstream license files, not a legal certification or a complete independent audit of every native library's embedded transitive source.
The synthetic SMS test dataset is not packaged in this app. Its CC BY 4.0 attribution is in the source repository's test resources README.
